# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Tech recruiters, hiring managers, and potential collaborators reviewing Ashley Wu's work. Context: quick screening on desktop, occasional mobile, ambient office or café light. Job: form a confident first impression, understand the work, decide whether to reach out. Emotional register: the visitor should feel like they've found something curated and singular — not another portfolio template.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Ashley Wu, CS junior at the University of Virginia, targeting Summer 2027 internships in systems and ML. The portfolio exists to demonstrate both technical depth and aesthetic sensibility — that Ashley builds things that are fast, accessible, and considered. Success: a recruiter or collaborator reaches out after visiting.

## Brand Personality

Atmospheric, precise, quietly confident. The design never announces itself — it simply is, and the right person will understand it. Like a naturalist's field notebook: handmade marks on good paper, annotation and record, evidence of a careful human mind.

## Anti-references

- **Dark SaaS / glassmorphism**: blue-purple gradients, frosted cards, Vercel-style dark mode, glow effects. This portfolio lives in warm parchment + ink, not OLED darkness.
- **Minimalist dev portfolio**: pure white background, mono font everywhere, GitHub-linked project list, no personality. The design must have a distinct voice.
- **Generic CS portfolio**: hero with headshot, skill icons in a row, 3-column project cards, resume PDF link above the fold.

## Design Principles

1. **Typography is the personality.** The contrast between large italic serif (Cormorant Garamond) and tiny monospace (Space Mono) carries the brand voice. Every section decision should serve this pairing.
2. **Marks, not decorations.** Add visual interest through symbolic marks — thin rules, registration glyphs, crosshairs, section numerals — not decorative flourishes or color variety.
3. **Quiet and deliberate.** When in doubt: reduce. The design should never perform. Atmospheric intimacy over visual loudness.
4. **Evidence of a human hand.** Asymmetry, overlap, and considered negative space. Things that could appear in a hand-assembled document.
5. **Scarcity of accent.** The red (`#CC3333`) is earned. Maximum 3 instances per page. Its rarity is what gives it meaning.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 against background. Large text ≥3:1. Reduced motion respected globally (already implemented via `prefers-reduced-motion`). Custom cursor degrades gracefully on touch devices.
